About Selar Technology Limited

Selar Technology Limited is a platform that connects people who need small tasks completed (“Employers”) with people who want to earn by completing verified online tasks (“Workers”). We focus on clear proof requirements, fair task rules, and a wallet-based payout experience designed for speed and transparency.

Our mission: Work should be simple and transparent

We want micro-work to be clear: set the task, define proof, verify outcomes, and pay fairly, without confusion.

Our approach: Proof-first execution

Tasks must include instructions and proof rules. Workers submit proof (text/link/screenshot), then employers review, approve, or dispute based on those rules.

Our promise: Safer micro-tasks

We use platform rules, anti-spam controls, and dispute handling to protect both employers and workers.

FAQ

Answers for Employers and Workers using Selar Technology Limited. If you still need help, email supportstl@yahoo.com.

For Workers

How do I start earning?

Create an account, browse available tasks, read the proof requirements carefully, complete the task, and submit proof. Once approved, earnings reflect in your wallet.

What counts as valid proof?

Proof depends on the task. Common examples: screenshots, links, text confirmations, or forms. Always follow the employer’s stated proof rules. Incomplete or unrelated proof can be rejected.

How fast is approval?

Approval time depends on the employer’s review flow. Some tasks may allow auto-approval after a set period, others require manual review.

How do withdrawals work?

Once your wallet has available funds, you can request a withdrawal to your linked payout method (e.g., bank transfer), subject to platform rules, verification, and anti-fraud checks.

Why was my submission rejected?

Common reasons: proof doesn’t match instructions, missing screenshots/links, low-quality evidence, or the task wasn’t completed as required. Review the task rules and resubmit if allowed.
